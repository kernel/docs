description: "Add concurrent browsers to your Start-Up plan, 25 at a time"
---

The Start-Up plan includes 150 [concurrent browsers](/browsers/concurrency-and-limits#concurrency). When you need more, add scaling packs. Each pack raises your limit by 25, up to 500 concurrent browsers.

| Each pack | |
| --- | --- |
| Concurrent browsers | +25 |
| [Browser create rate](/browsers/concurrency-and-limits#rate-limits) | +10 per minute |
| Price | \$100 per month, recurring |

Packs renew automatically every month until you remove them. You can add up to 14 packs. If you need more than 500 concurrent browsers, [contact sales](/info/contact-sales) about the Enterprise plan.

## Availability

Scaling packs are available to organizations on an active Start-Up plan that pay by card. Only organization admins can add or remove packs.

Organizations in a trial, and organizations billed through the [Vercel Marketplace](/integrations/vercel/marketplace), can't add packs.

<Note>
  Scaling packs are rolling out gradually. If you don't see them on your billing page and want to be opted in, [email support@kernel.sh](mailto:support@kernel.sh?subject=Scaling%20packs%20access).
</Note>

## Add packs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Go to the [KERNEL billing page](https://dashboard.onkernel.com/billing) as an organization admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your new limit">
    In the **Subscription** card, select **Raise max concurrency**. If you already have packs, the button reads **Change max concurrency**. Pick a limit from 175 to 500, in steps of 25. The dashboard shows what you'll pay today and your new monthly total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select the **Raise to** button, which shows the limit you picked. Your new limit is available within 15 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You pay for new packs when you add them, prorated for the rest of the month. After that, each pack is charged at \$100 every month until you remove it.

For example, add two packs halfway through the month and you pay about \$100 that day, then \$200 each month after.

## Remove packs

On the billing page, select **Lower max concurrency** or **Change max concurrency** and choose a lower limit. Choose 150 to remove every pack.

- **The change takes effect on the first of next month at 00:00 utc.** You keep your current limit until then.
- **There's no refund or credit for the current month.** Your next bill reflects the lower number of packs.

Until the change takes effect, you can cancel it with **Cancel decrease** or choose a different limit. If you choose a limit higher than the one you have now, that's a new purchase: you pay for the added packs that day and get the higher limit right away.

## Browser pools

A [browser pool](/browsers/pools) reserves its full size against your concurrent browser limit. If your pools reserve more than your new limit when packs are removed, KERNEL reduces every pool proportionally so they fit.

Browsers you're using aren't interrupted. They keep running until you release them.

To choose which pools get smaller, [resize them](/browsers/pools) yourself before the first of the month.

## Changing plans

Packs are part of the Start-Up plan, so they end when you leave it.

| If you | Your packs | Your browser pools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downgrade | Work as usual until the downgrade takes effect, then end. You don't need to remove them first. | Reduced to fit your new plan's [limit](/browsers/concurrency-and-limits#concurrency), with or without packs. |
| Upgrade to Enterprise | End when your Enterprise plan starts. Your contract's limits apply instead. | Not changed. |

## Usage above 150 browsers

Your plan's [usage rates](/info/pricing#usage-rates) cover your first 150 concurrent browsers. Browsers you run above 150 are billed at the overage rate, which is 1.5 times the standard rate.

| Usage type | Standard ($/sec) | Above 150 ($/sec) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headful browser | 0.0001333336 | 0.0002 |
| Headless browser | 0.0000166667 | 0.000025 |
| Headful browser + GPU acceleration | 0.0008000016 | 0.0012 |

App invocation rates don't change. If you don't have packs, your limit is 150 and the overage rate never applies.

## FAQ

<Accordion title="How do I check my current limit?">
  The billing page shows your current limit. You can also run [`kernel org limits get`](/reference/cli/org#kernel-org-limits-get).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do packs change my project limits?">
  No. Packs raise your organization's limit. [Project concurrency limits](/info/projects#concurrency-limits) you've set stay as they are.
</Accordion>
